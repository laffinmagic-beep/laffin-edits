My Story

Why I Started Laffin Edits.

I perform magic. And for a long time, I watched other magicians struggle to find editors who actually understood what they were doing on stage. Editors who didn't know why a pause matters. Who didn't understand that cutting away at the wrong moment could expose a method.

So I started editing my own footage. And then editing for other magicians. And what I found was that my background as a performer changed every single decision I made in the edit room — in ways that a regular editor simply couldn't replicate.

Laffin Edits was built on that foundation. Every project I take on — whether it's a magic reel, a corporate promo, a YouTube channel, or a social media package — gets the same performance-first approach. I edit every project with the same attention to timing, reactions, and detail I'd want for my own performance videos.

"This isn't just a video editing service. It's editing that understands magic — and performance — from the inside."

The Mission

Why It Matters.

Magic is one of the hardest things in the world to capture on video. The live experience — the tension, the reactions, the moment of astonishment — is what makes magic work. Most video editing strips that away. Bad cuts, wrong angles, missed reactions. The magic gets lost.

The mission behind Laffin Edits is simple — protect the magic. Whether you're a working magician building your booking reel, a performer growing your social media, a business that needs a polished promo, or a creator who needs consistent monthly content — the goal is always the same. Make a video that works. That represents you. That gets results.

"You'll always have input. We work together until it's right — because the video represents you, not my editing style."
