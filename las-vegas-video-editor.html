Las Vegas

Video editor in Las Vegas for people who actually perform

Laffin Edits · Cory Laffin · (702) 763-8683

I live here. I work here. A lot of the footage I cut is from rooms on the Strip, downtown, or a suite someone rented for the weekend. I also take jobs from out of town. The edit is the same either way.

If you search “Las Vegas video editor” you will get production companies and wedding crews. That is fine if you need a camera package. If you already shot the thing and you need someone who knows when a trick is about to flash, that is a different job.

What I cut

Performer promos. Social clips. Brand spots when a business wants something that does not feel like a template. Monthly batches if you need to stay visible without living in Premiere.

Social starts at $150. A promo starts at $500. Four clips a month starts at $499.

Why the local part matters

Vegas rooms are loud and ugly to record in. Phone footage. House lights. Someone walking through the shot. I cut around that all the time. I do not need a perfect set to make a promo you can send a booker.

I also will not leave in an angle that gives the method away. That is the part most editors miss because they are not in the act.

Send what you have. Even if it is last weekend on your phone. Tell me if this is for a website, Instagram, or both.
