Why It Matters

Editing That Understands Magic.

Magic videos are the hardest performance content to edit. Timing, pauses, reactions, and misdirection matter just as much on screen as they do live. A bad cut doesn't just look off — it can kill a moment or expose a method.

I'm a working magician, not just an editor. I understand why a pause exists, when a reaction should breathe, and what absolutely cannot be shown. That same instinct for performance carries into every project I take on.
