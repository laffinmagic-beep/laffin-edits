Process

The Process

Once we’ve discussed your project, you can send footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or a similar service. MP4 and MOV are usually best, but I can look at what you have and let you know if there are any file issues.

Turnaround depends on the footage, project size, video length, revision needs, and current schedule. Once I see what you’re working with, I’ll give you a realistic estimate and keep communication clear throughout the project.

Every project includes 2 revision rounds. That's usually all it takes — and if something specific needs adjusting beyond that we'll figure it out together. The goal is always a final video you're genuinely happy with.

Final files are usually delivered as MP4, which works well for most platforms. If you need a specific format, resolution, or aspect ratio, let me know before export so it can be prepared correctly.
