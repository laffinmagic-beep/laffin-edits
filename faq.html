Process

The Process

Once we've discussed your project, you can send footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or any similar service. Just about any format works — MP4, MOV, AVI. If there are any issues with your files I'll let you know right away.

Most projects receive a first draft within a few days of receiving all footage. Turnaround time depends on the scope and complexity of the project. I keep communication open throughout so you always know exactly where things stand — no disappearing, no guessing.

Every project includes 2 revision rounds. That's usually all it takes — and if something specific needs adjusting beyond that we'll figure it out together. The goal is always a final video you're genuinely happy with.

Final files are delivered in the format you need — MP4 is standard and works everywhere. If you need a specific format, resolution, or aspect ratio for a particular platform or use case just let me know and I'll deliver it ready to use.
